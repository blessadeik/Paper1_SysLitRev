\subsection{Quality Assessment Tool}

Seven-item methodological quality checklist (0--7 scale; see Section~\ref{sec:methodology} for definitions):

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Research question clearly stated (1 point)
    \item Participant characteristics sufficiently described (1 point)
    \item Neural recording paradigm reproducible (1 point)
    \item AI architecture described for replication (1 point)
    \item Primary metric pre-specified with variability measures (1 point)
    \item Performance compared to baseline (1 point)
    \item Risk of overestimation discussed/mitigated (1 point)
\end{enumerate}

Quality classification: High ($\geq 5.5$), Moderate (3.5--5.0), Low ($<3.5$)
